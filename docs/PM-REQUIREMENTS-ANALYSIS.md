## 2. Detailed Breakdown by Role Archetype

### A. Technical Product Manager (TPM) / Platform PO
* **Fit Level: VERY STRONG**
* **Why:** 7 years inside carrier-grade telecom and embedded network systems (Nokia, CTDI, Radio Design). Strong technical fluency with engineers, APIs, system architectures, database schemas, and edge constraints.
* **Key Evidence:** SM1660 Matrix E, GPON cards, Experiment Tracker database integrity tests, ReviewFlow evaluator scripts.
* **Gaps to Address:** Experience with cloud distributed microservices (AWS/GCP/Kubernetes) is demonstrated through personal code rather than enterprise production deployments.

### B. Associate Product Manager (APM) / Early-Career PM
* **Fit Level: STRONG**
* **Why:** Demonstrates complete end-to-end product thinking that exceeds standard junior candidates: 3 comprehensive flagship projects with working code and live prototypes, not just slide decks or wireframe sketches.
* **Key Evidence:** Full PRDs, 8-stage lifecycle alignment, RICE backlogs, decision records.
* **Gaps to Address:** Interviewers will test candidate on real-world stakeholder conflict resolution and handling shifting executive mandates.

### C. AI Product Manager / Prompt & Evaluation Engineer
* **Fit Level: STRONG**
* **Why:** Understands the critical distinction between generative demos and production evaluation. Built automated grading suites measuring rating alignment and hallucination drift in ReviewFlow.
* **Key Evidence:** IIM PM in Agentic AI Era, ReviewFlow 15-case regression suite and evaluator, ScholarHat Python for AI.
* **Gaps to Address:** Fine-tuning large open models or RLHF workflows at scale.

### D. Senior Growth / Commercial PM
* **Fit Level: CURRENTLY LIMITED**
* **Why:** These roles require proven multi-million dollar revenue acceleration, customer acquisition cost (CAC) optimization, and enterprise pricing negotiation.
* **Positioning Advice:** Focus candidacies strictly on APM, PM, TPM, PO, and Product Analyst roles where systems thinking, analytical rigor, and execution integrity are valued over historical P&L ownership.
