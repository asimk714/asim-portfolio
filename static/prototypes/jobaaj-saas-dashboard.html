Target Personas & Pain Solvers

Designing SaaS interfaces around specific goals and critical funnel leaks.

A

Amit

The Tech Transitioner (Nokia Solutions / Ex-Telecom)

Goals:

Move from telecom engineering to Product Management. Seeks credentials verifying SQL, metrics, and PRD drafting.

Pain Points:

Theoretical video fatigue. Lacks sandbox tool access, completing standard certificates without writing code or building mock funnels.

SaaS Solver: Immediate drops into the SQL console to complete onboarding, creating verifiable project exports linking to LinkedIn.
P

Priya

The Independent Instructor

Goals:

Seamlessly publish and monetize specialized courses without complex setups or high commission cuts.

Pain Points:

Platforms like Teachable and Kajabi lack custom sandbox APIs to build interactive coding and analytical labs for students.

SaaS Solver: Self-serve course outliners and custom SQL terminal templates to structure course environments.
R

Raj

Corporate L&D Manager

Goals:

Deploy consistent, trackable department-wide upskilling with compliance-ready certifications.

Pain Points:

Self-paced video courses lack cohort reporting. Cannot verify actual team capabilities without centralized logs.

SaaS Solver: Multi-tenant Business Dashboards with compliance-auditable grading tables and group progression logs.
