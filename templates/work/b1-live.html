Walk through the transition workflow

Enterprise AI Pilot Transition is a product strategy framework for governing the path from AI pilot to production. This interactive simulator demonstrates the proposed 4-stage sequence: a pilot team initiates transfer, readiness and governance/security checkpoints are validated, production ownership is confirmed, and the outcome is either accepted or sent back with clear remediation requirements.

Illustrative demonstration only. No real enterprises, pilots, or outcomes are involved.

Pilot context

This is an illustrative pilot — not a real enterprise. The purpose is to show what the workflow surfaces at each step, not to demonstrate a real transition.

Illustrative pilot: A business-unit team has built a promising internal AI assistant prototype. There is no clear production owner ready to absorb it. The team wants a path to a governed operational workflow — or an honest send-back.

Framework scope

What the workflow covers

  • Readiness assessment / confirmation
  • Governance / security / internal-policy checkpoint
  • Production ownership transfer
  • Minimal accountability handoff
  • Escalation / handoff context

This framework does NOT own model development, deployment infrastructure, monitoring-as-primary-product, enterprise security/compliance programs, ITSM replacement, business workflow automation, or end-to-end AI lifecycle management. The boundary is what keeps this a bounded transition workflow.

Moment of value

Accept or send back

The moment of value is production ownership acceptance OR explicit send-back with actionable readiness gaps — not "the pilot is live in production," which is outside the framework's boundary.
