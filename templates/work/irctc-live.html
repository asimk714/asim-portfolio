PM Assignment: Concurrency vs. UX

IRCTC (India's railway reservation platform) faces extreme concurrency during Tatkal booking windows (10:00 AM and 11:00 AM). The prototype frames this as a concurrency-vs-UX problem: database load, layout shifts from async ad banners, and session drop-off degrade the checkout experience at the worst possible moment.
