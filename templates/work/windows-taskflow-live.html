How to try it

  1. Click desktop icons or taskbar buttons to launch application windows.
  2. Drag windows by their titlebars to arrange your multi-app layout.
  3. Type a context name (e.g. "Q3 Revenue Review") and click Save.
  4. Close or rearrange apps, then click Restore to instantly recover the workspace context.
