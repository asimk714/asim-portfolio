Thinking out loud, with evidence labels.

I distinguish what I've observed, what I've verified, what I've inferred, what's a hypothesis, what's proposed, and what's not evidenced. This isn't academic — it's how I avoid overclaiming. In my case studies, you'll see which claims are backed by tests, which are inferred, and which are things I explicitly haven't measured yet.

AI Product Principle

AI can improve how you say it. It cannot decide what happened.

The core principle from ReviewFlow. The AI's job is wording, not judgment. Rating preservation as a hard constraint. Facts only from user input. This single line is the throughline of the portfolio.

Verification Integrity

Verify what you claim. Say openly what you haven't validated.

Experiment Tracker's 17-category verification report. ReviewFlow's corrected evaluator and honest intervention record. The evidence-label system runs through all of it: OBSERVED, VERIFIED, INFERRED, HYPOTHESIS, PROPOSED, NOT EVIDENCED.
