The three projects on this portfolio are the proof that this way of working transferred into product work. Each one is a real product I built end-to-end — with its own measurement, verification, and honest gaps. They are not hypotheticals. They run.

Context

Customer site repeatedly raised outage-related tickets. After the issue persisted through initial troubleshooting, the team replaced hardware and returned the suspected card to the repair centre.

Investigation

Recreated the field environment at the repair centre and compared unit behaviour between the field site and the controlled environment. The investigation identified sudden power surges causing network cards to reboot.

Stakeholders

Customer-handling team, Site Reliability Engineers, relevant stakeholders and managers in the escalation path, and repair-centre technical personnel.

Decision

I participated in the investigation and recommendation. I proposed an alternate continuous-power solution. I did not implement the power infrastructure myself.

Outcome

The same customer has not raised the same complaint in roughly two years — based on my experience and reporting, not independently measured in this portfolio.

Framing: engineering/customer escalation and RCA experience — not formal Product Manager ownership.

Context

GPON Fixed Network system — a site kept going down after the GPON ticket was raised. I was involved from the beginning because I regularly handle customer escalations and RCA.

Investigation

I proposed bringing the complete field setup to the Repair Center with the available diagnostic outputs. The investigation established that an NT card was resetting — which could corrupt the flash card holding configuration, ultimately causing the FX-8 shelf to reset. At the Repair Center, I first reproduced the field configuration and then isolated individual boards when the setup initially appeared healthy. Under the field configuration, I observed a slight reset on one NT card, replaced it, and confirmed that the setup then operated normally.

Stakeholders

Customer-handling team, SRE, technical personnel, and the Repair Center. The operational workflow follows a clear chain: SRE establishes a site and checks whether it is running smoothly; when a problem occurs, the SRE escalates to the relevant stakeholders or managers depending on the issue and the affected board or component.

Resolution

Fault finding, root-cause analysis (RCA), troubleshooting, and dispatching OK / working units. The work spans both hardware and software. I have strong knowledge of the hardware, and when site problems, hardware problems, or repeated issues occur, people contact me because of that hardware knowledge.

Operational context: GPON site tickets and returned-unit history are recorded through the operational process, with DOA, repaired-faulty, and repeat-failure cases receiving deeper investigation. This example documents the observed investigation and repair action; it does not claim that I personally performed a formal long-term recurrence analysis for this site.

Framing: engineering/RCA and cross-functional coordination — not formal Product Manager ownership.
