How I Think

From ambiguity to something verifiable.

I define products before I build them, measure before I claim, and verify what I ship. Then I'm honest about what I haven't validated yet.

Problem-first

I sit with a vague brief and turn it into a product question engineers can build against — usually before the first line of code. The problem is the product.

Trade-offs, not checklists

Every product decision is a choice between options with real costs. I make the trade-offs explicit and record the "why not" for what I leave out.

Measure before claim

I define what success means before I say I achieved it. Where I can't measure, I say so — because otherwise the claim isn't worth trusting.

Engineering fluency

8+ years inside complex technical delivery means I can read a system, talk to engineers about constraints, and tell the difference between a measured outcome and a claim I've talked myself into.
